# 🚀 Digital Productivity Assistant

[🇧🇷 Português](README.md) | 🇺🇸 English | [🇪🇸 Español](README.es.md)

> A conversational assistant for digital productivity, helping users with documents, spreadsheets, formulas, corrections, tables, ready-to-use answers, and data interpretation.

## 📌 About the project

**Digital Productivity Assistant** is a project under development designed to help people solve specific problems related to digital productivity tools.

The idea emerged from recurring situations in which people requested help solving computer-related problems, ranging from questions about documents and spreadsheets to more specific technical demands. These recurring needs also led to personalized online consulting services.

By observing that many questions followed similar patterns, the idea emerged to build a simple and autonomous tool capable of identifying a user's need through questions, collecting additional context, and delivering a practical solution.

The project may also support simplified educational versions and personalized consulting workflows.

## 🎯 Objective

Help users solve a specific problem without requiring them to master an entire software application.

The assistant aims to:

- identify the need;
- understand context;
- ask follow-up questions;
- provide formulas and examples;
- correct or explain solutions;
- organize information;
- generate tables;
- produce ready-to-use answers;
- interpret data;
- provide step-by-step guidance.

## 🧩 Initial MVP

The first version starts with a structured question flow that identifies the tool and type of problem before collecting the necessary details.

## 📊 Spreadsheet assistant

Potential topics include lookup functions, logical formulas, conditional calculations, filters, data organization, cleaning, indicators, charts, and result interpretation.

The assistant may explain both the formula and why it solves the user's specific problem.

## 📝 Document assistant

Potential topics include formatting, styles, headings, paragraphs, spacing, tables, content organization, structural review, text improvement, templates, and ready-to-use responses.

## 📈 Data interpretation

The assistant may help identify trends, compare periods, calculate changes, suggest indicators, summarize results, and transform raw data into understandable conclusions.

## 🧠 Design principle

> **People often do not need to learn an entire tool at that moment. They need to solve a specific problem.**
